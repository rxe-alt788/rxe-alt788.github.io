01 / Environmental decision support↗

4NICO

Conditions are not confidence.

A beach-management decision-support framework that separates environmental state from observation confidence. It does not predict attacks or declare beaches safe.

Phase 0 synthetic baseline: 68.9% alert occupancy; 12.83 transitions per month after a 24-hour de-escalation hold. Phase 1 observed BOM rainfall is in validation. These are engineering metrics, not model-accuracy results.

Phase 1 validation in progress
