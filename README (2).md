# CivicOS

**Smart cities aren't finished. Their best uses haven't been invented yet.**

Smart city infrastructure was installed to do one job. It can do many more. Finding those uses is an act of liberation and creativity, not procurement. You don't buy a smarter city. You evolve the one you have, and the better uses appear as you do.

CivicOS came from a discussion with local women's safety groups. We talked about how sharing our data could make us safer in the city, and workshopped open-source solutions with the City of Newcastle. Nobody commissioned it. It doesn't need anyone to.

**What it is:** a lightweight layer that helps a city's existing systems talk to each other, interpret data in civic terms, and act on it in real time, with humans on the loop and visible feedback that earns public trust. No new hardware.

**7 days. Data and decisions.**
